About

Founded in Charleston in 1904. Expanded to UCSB in 2025. Colors: white, gold, and royal blue.

89 Members, Winter 2026
3.326 Average chapter GPA
540+ Service hours, Spring Quarter 2026

Pi Kappa Phi was founded at the College of Charleston in 1904.

In 2025, Pi Kappa Phi officially expanded to UC Santa Barbara, where the Alpha Class began establishing Lambda Delta.

Rush is held at the start of fall and spring quarter. Check UCSB IFC for more information.
